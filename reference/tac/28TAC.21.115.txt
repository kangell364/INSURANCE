§21.115. Rules Pertaining Specifically to Property and Casualty Insurance
Advertising.

(a) No advertisement may use the word "dividends" or similar words or
illustrations in such a manner as to state or to imply that future dividends
are guaranteed or certain to occur.

(b) The word "dividends" includes every return of premium and payment to
policyholders on a particular policy that is predicated on the financial
performance or earnings of the insurer, but does not include the return of
premium under a nondiscretionary provision or endorsement in a policy clearly
providing for the payment under a rating plan approved or promulgated by the
Texas Department of Insurance.
