### An insurer receives notice of a hail claim on 1 June. By when must it acknowledge and begin investigating?

- [x] By 16 June — not later than the 15th day after receiving notice
- [ ] By the 15th business day after receiving notice
- [ ] By 1 July — the 30th day after receiving notice
- [ ] By the fifth business day after receiving notice

**§542.055(a)** requires three things within that period: **acknowledge receipt,
commence any investigation, and request everything the insurer reasonably
believes at that time it will need.** It is **15 calendar days** — an eligible
surplus lines insurer gets the 30th business day.

### The insurer receives the final proof of loss on 1 July and needs longer than the statutory period. What must it do?

- [x] Notify the claimant within the period of the reasons it needs more time, then decide by the 45th day after that notice
- [ ] Simply take the additional time, up to 60 days
- [ ] Obtain the claimant's written consent to an extension
- [ ] Issue a partial payment and continue investigating

**§542.056(d)** — the notice must come **within the original period**, not
afterwards. Missing that step is what turns a slow claim into a penalty claim.

### The insurer notifies acceptance on 20 July. By when must it pay?

- [x] Within 5 business days of the notice
- [ ] Within 15 business days of the notice
- [ ] Within 30 days of the notice
- [ ] Within 60 days of the notice

**§542.057(a)**. Where payment is conditioned on the claimant performing an act,
the five business days run from **performance of the act**; an eligible surplus
lines insurer has **20 business days**.

### The insurer has a reasonable basis to believe the loss resulted from arson. How long does it have to accept or reject?

- [x] The 30th day after receiving all required items — calendar days, not business days
- [ ] The 15th business day, as for any other claim
- [ ] The 45th day after receiving all required items
- [ ] The 60th day after receiving notice of the claim

**§542.056(b)**. The ordinary rule is the **15th business day** after receiving
everything needed for final proof of loss; arson moves it to **30 calendar
days**.

### Payment is 90 days late on a $120,000 claim, with no Chapter 542A issue. What does §542.060(a) add?

- [x] 18% a year interest on the amount of the claim as damages, plus reasonable and necessary attorney's fees
- [ ] 18% of the claim as a one-off penalty
- [ ] Treble damages, as under Chapter 541
- [ ] Interest at the judgment rate only

The damages are **in addition to the amount of the claim**, and where suit is
filed the fees are **taxed as part of the costs**.

### A pedestrian sues the insured and complains the liability insurer is slow. Does Subchapter B help them?

- [x] No — the Prompt Payment of Claims Act applies to first-party claims
- [ ] Yes, any claimant may invoke the deadlines
- [ ] Yes, but only after judgment against the insured
- [ ] Yes, with the deadlines doubled for liability claims

**First-party is the whole scope.** §542.051(2) defines a claim as a first-party
claim under a policy covering real or personal property, or a first-party death,
disability or income claim.

### After receiving everything it reasonably requested, how long may an insurer delay payment before §542.060 damages attach?

- [x] 60 days
- [ ] 45 days
- [ ] 30 days
- [ ] 90 days

**§542.058(a)** sets the outer limit. **§542.058(b)** disapplies it where
arbitration or litigation finds the claim **invalid and not payable**.

### What does §542.059(b) do in a weather-related catastrophe or major natural disaster?

- [x] Extends the claim-handling deadlines by an additional 15 days
- [ ] Suspends the deadlines until the Commissioner lifts the designation
- [ ] Doubles every deadline in the subchapter
- [ ] Waives the 18% damages for claims in the affected area

The catastrophe must be one **defined by the commissioner**. Separately,
§542.059(a) lets a court extend the periods for a **guaranty association** on a
showing of good cause after notice to policyholders.

### What changed in §542.060 in 2017?

- [x] Subsection (c) sets a different rate — five percent added to the Finance Code §304.003 rate — for actions to which Chapter 542A applies
- [ ] The 18% rate was repealed entirely
- [ ] Attorney's fees ceased to be recoverable
- [ ] The rate rose from 12% to 18%

**18% is the §542.060(a) answer and the one an exam is most likely to want — but
it is no longer the rate in every case**, and material written before H.B. 1774
does not know that. The Chapter 542A interest accrues **from the date the claim
was required to be paid**.

### If an insurer's acknowledgment of a claim is not in writing, what must it do?

- [x] Record the date, manner and content of the acknowledgment
- [ ] Follow it with a written acknowledgment within five business days
- [ ] Obtain the claimant's signature confirming the call
- [ ] Nothing; oral acknowledgment satisfies the statute outright

**§542.055(c)**. The requirement exists so that compliance with the 15-day clock
is provable later.

### Must a rejection of a claim state the reasons for it?

- [x] Yes — §542.056(c) requires it
- [ ] No, only an acceptance must be explained
- [ ] Only where the claimant requests reasons in writing
- [ ] Only where the claim exceeds $25,000

Notice of acceptance or rejection must be **in writing** under §542.056(a), and a
rejection must carry its reasons.

### A claimant alleges both a prompt-payment violation and a Chapter 541 unfair practice on the same facts. Is that permitted?

- [x] Yes — §542.061 makes these remedies additional to any other remedy at law or common law
- [ ] No, the claimant must elect one chapter
- [ ] Only where the insurer acted knowingly
- [ ] Only where the Department has first found a violation

Where a question asks which chapter prohibits an unfair **settlement** practice,
**both do** — §541.060 and §542.003 — and §541 is the one carrying the private
action and treble damages.

### Which deadline is measured in calendar days rather than business days?

- [x] The 15-day acknowledge-and-investigate deadline
- [ ] The accept-or-reject deadline
- [ ] The deadline to pay after notifying acceptance
- [ ] The surplus lines acknowledgment deadline

**15 / 15 / 5 — acknowledge, decide, pay.** The first is calendar; the second and
third are business days. §542.051(1) defines a business day as a day other than
Saturday, Sunday or a state-recognised holiday.
