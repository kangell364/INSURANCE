### A homeowner in Galveston cannot get windstorm coverage. Which association, and how many declinations?

- [x] TWIA, on one declination
- [ ] The FAIR Plan, on two declinations
- [ ] TWIA, on two declinations
- [ ] The FAIR Plan, on one declination

**One declination versus two is the contrast most likely to be tested.** For TWIA
renewal, a declination is required only **every three calendar years**.

### A homeowner in an inland underserved area cannot get any residential property insurance. Which association, and how many declinations?

- [x] The FAIR Plan, on two declinations from insurers authorised to write and actually writing residential property insurance
- [ ] TWIA, on one declination
- [ ] The FAIR Plan, on one declination
- [ ] Neither; the homeowner must use surplus lines

Note the same **"authorized and actually writing"** qualifier met in surplus
lines — an insurer holding the authority but out of the market does not count.

### Could that inland homeowner get wind and hail from the FAIR Plan if their property were in a first-tier coastal county?

- [x] No — §2211.156 bars the FAIR Plan from covering wind and hail eligible under Chapter 2210
- [ ] Yes, the two plans overlap on the coast
- [ ] Yes, but only above the TWIA maximum limit
- [ ] Yes, with the commissioner's approval

**Wind and hail on the coast is TWIA. Everything else that the voluntary market
will not write is the FAIR Plan. They do not overlap.**

### Rain is driven into a TWIA-insured dwelling by hurricane winds, without the wind making an opening. Covered?

- [x] Yes — §2210.208(a) requires cover regardless of whether an opening is made by the wind
- [ ] No, an opening made by wind is required
- [ ] Only if the interior damage exceeds the deductible twice over
- [ ] Only where flood insurance is also in force

**A deliberate departure from the usual property form**, which requires an
opening, and a reliable exam point.

### A TWIA policyholder believes the association handled their claim in bad faith and wants treble damages under Chapter 541. Will that work?

- [x] No — §2210.014 and §2210.572 bar a Chapter 541 or 542 action, and §541.152(c) excludes TWIA from treble damages
- [ ] Yes, on proof the association acted knowingly
- [ ] Yes, but capped at actual damages plus attorney's fees
- [ ] Only after exhausting the association's internal appeal

**The statute provides its own claim procedure instead.** A fact pattern inviting
you to apply the ordinary bad-faith remedies to a windstorm claim is testing
exactly this.

### A FAIR Plan insured wants to sue the member insurers who fund the plan. May they?

- [x] No — §2211.055(b) makes the association the proper party for all purposes
- [ ] Yes, each member insurer is liable for its participation share
- [ ] Yes, but only after judgment against the association
- [ ] Only where the association is insolvent

Notice, proof of loss and legal process must all be served **on the association**,
and §2211.055(c) requires the policy to say so.

### TWIA damage occurs on 1 March 2026. By when must the claim be filed?

- [x] By 1 March 2027, the first anniversary, extendable for good cause up to 180 days
- [ ] Within 60 days of the damage
- [ ] Within two years of the damage
- [ ] Within 90 days of the damage

§2210.205(a)(1) and §2210.573(a). Separately, **§2210.573(d)** requires TWIA to
notify the claimant **in writing within 60 days** that it accepts in full, accepts
and denies in part, or denies in full.

### How is TWIA membership determined?

- [x] Compulsory for all property insurers authorised to write property insurance in Texas, as a condition of their authority
- [ ] Voluntary, with a premium credit for participating insurers
- [ ] Limited to insurers writing in the seacoast territory
- [ ] Determined annually by the commissioner by rule

**The insurers that decline the risk collectively carry it.** §2210.001 also
requires TWIA to function so as **not to be a direct competitor in the private
market**.

### A TWIA-insured dwelling is covered for 85% of its full replacement cost. How is a partial loss settled?

- [x] At replacement cost of the damaged part — §2210.207(c) applies at 80% or more
- [ ] At actual cash value, since the property is not fully insured
- [ ] At 85% of the replacement cost of the damaged part
- [ ] At replacement cost only if the loss is total

**The insurance-to-value rule from Module 1, written into statute.** Below 80%,
§2210.207(d) limits liability as that section provides.

### What is the TWIA maximum limit for a dwelling and its contents?

- [x] $350,000, adjusted annually for inflation by the BOECKH Index
- [ ] $250,000, fixed by statute
- [ ] $1,500,000, the same as any other structure
- [ ] $2,192,000

The figures move, so **learn their shape and order of magnitude**: $350,000 for a
dwelling, **$125,000** for individually owned contents in an apartment,
condominium or townhouse unit, **$1,500,000** for any other structure and its
contents, and **$2,192,000** for a building at least 75% occupied by a
governmental entity.

### On what condition may the commissioner establish the FAIR Plan?

- [x] After a public hearing, on finding residential property insurance is not reasonably available, or that at least 25% of qualified market-assistance applicants went unplaced in the preceding six months
- [ ] On the request of ten or more member insurers
- [ ] Automatically, whenever TWIA's exposure exceeds a statutory trigger
- [ ] On a declaration of disaster by the Governor

**§2211.051(a)**, and **§2211.152** requires the commissioner to designate the
**underserved areas** by rule.

### Who sits on the FAIR Plan governing committee?

- [x] Eleven members appointed by the commissioner: five insurer representatives, four public members and two general property and casualty agents
- [ ] Nine members elected by participating insurers
- [ ] Eleven members: five public, four insurer and two agents
- [ ] Seven members appointed by the Governor

The commissioner or a designated employee serves **ex officio**, and the
commissioner may **remove a member without cause**.

### A structure built in 2015 in Flood Zone V applies to TWIA. What is required?

- [x] Flood insurance, as a condition of the windstorm policy
- [ ] Two declinations rather than one
- [ ] An inspection by the FAIR Plan inspection bureau
- [ ] Nothing beyond the ordinary eligibility requirements

**§2210.203(a-1)** applies to structures built or altered **on or after
1 September 2009** in Flood Zone V.

### How long does the commissioner have to approve or disapprove FAIR Plan rates?

- [x] 60 days, extendable by 30 days on notice, and no policy may be issued until rates are approved
- [ ] 30 days, with no extension
- [ ] 90 days, after which rates are deemed approved
- [ ] There is no deadline

**§2211.056(b)** requires rates sufficient to **carry all claims to maturity** and
meet expenses.

### Are wind turbines insurable by TWIA?

- [x] No — §2210.210 excludes them
- [ ] Yes, as other structures up to $1,500,000
- [ ] Yes, but only at actual cash value
- [ ] Yes, where the owner also insures a dwelling with TWIA

TWIA also must **accept credit cards and offer instalments** under §2210.2032,
and a renewal payment grace period of **not more than 10 days** applies under
§2210.203(c-1).
