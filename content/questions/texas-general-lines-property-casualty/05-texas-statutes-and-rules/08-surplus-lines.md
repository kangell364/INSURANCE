### What does Texas require of a surplus lines agent regarding an insurer's finances?

- [x] A reasonable effort to determine its financial condition, and no knowing placement with a financially unsound insurer
- [ ] Confirmation that it holds an A.M. Best rating of A- or better
- [ ] Nothing — the Department reviews solvency before granting eligibility
- [ ] Written consent from the insured acknowledging the insurer's rating

**§981.211** states the duty in exactly those terms: subsection (a) requires a
**reasonable effort to determine the financial condition** before placing, and
subsection (b) forbids **knowingly placing with a financially unsound
insurer.**

No particular rating or grade is prescribed. A current rating from A.M. Best
or a similar agency is the ordinary way an agent discharges the duty — it is
evidence, not the standard itself.

The third option is precisely backwards, and the surplus lines document must
say so in 11-point type: **the Department does not audit the finances or
review the solvency** of a surplus lines insurer, and the policy has **no
guaranty association** behind it. That is why the duty falls on the agent.

### A general lines agent finds an admitted market will write only $2m of a $5m exposure. What may they place in surplus lines?

- [x] Only the $3m that exceeds what the admitted market will write
- [ ] The full $5m, replacing the admitted quote
- [ ] Nothing; a partial admitted market defeats the placement
- [ ] The full $5m, provided the admitted insurer consents

**§981.004(b) — it tops up; it does not replace.** Surplus lines may provide
coverage only in the amount exceeding what is obtainable from authorized
insurers.

### On those facts, what licence does the agent need?

- [x] A surplus lines licence in addition to the general lines licence
- [ ] The general lines licence alone
- [ ] A non-resident licence for the insurer's domicile
- [ ] A managing general agent licence

**§981.202** — a Texas-licensed agent may not issue or cause to be issued a
contract with an eligible surplus lines insurer unless they hold a surplus lines
licence. **This is the single most likely licensing question on the topic.**

### An admitted insurer holds authority for the class but stopped writing it two years ago. Does it count for the diligent effort?

- [x] No — the insurer must be both authorized to write and actually writing the class
- [ ] Yes, holding the authority is sufficient
- [ ] Yes, unless the insurer has formally withdrawn the filing
- [ ] Only where the insurer declines in writing

Both qualifiers in §981.004(a)(1) do real work. **An insurer that holds the
authority but has stopped writing the class is not an available market.**

### A surplus lines insurer becomes insolvent. Does the Texas guaranty association pay the claim?

- [x] No — the insurer is not a member of the association created under Chapter 462
- [ ] Yes, on the same terms as an admitted insurer's policyholders
- [ ] Yes, up to half the claim
- [ ] Only for personal lines policies

**That is the real price of the wider market**, and the document must say so in
11-point type under §981.101(b).

### What is the minimum capital and surplus for an eligible surplus lines insurer, and who is exempt?

- [x] $15 million, with alien insurers on the NAIC Quarterly Listing exempt
- [ ] $5 million, with no exemptions
- [ ] $25 million, with domestic insurers exempt
- [ ] $15 million, with no exemptions

**§981.057(a)** sets the figure; **§981.057(b)** exempts alien surplus lines
insurers listed on the **Quarterly Listing of Alien Insurers** maintained by the
NAIC's International Insurers Department.

### By when must the agent file the policy with the stamping office?

- [x] Not later than the 60th day after the later of the effective date or the issue date
- [ ] Not later than the 30th day after the effective date
- [ ] Not later than the 15th business day after the issue date
- [ ] At the end of the calendar quarter

**§981.105(a)**, and **§981.214** requires the agent to comply with the stamping
office's plan of operation.

### A large manufacturer wants a surplus lines placement without the agent canvassing the admitted market. Possible?

- [x] Yes, if it is an exempt commercial purchaser, after disclosure and a written request
- [ ] No, the diligent effort requirement admits no exception
- [ ] Yes, for any commercial risk above $1 million
- [ ] Yes, with the Commissioner's written approval

**§981.004(c)** — the agent must disclose that comparable insurance may be
available from the admitted market, which is **subject to more regulatory
oversight** and **may provide greater protection**, and must receive the
purchaser's **written request** to place it in surplus lines anyway.

### Which purpose of Chapter 981 do candidates most often miss?

- [x] Protecting authorized insurers from unfair competition by unauthorized insurers
- [ ] Providing orderly access to eligible surplus lines insurers
- [ ] Ensuring fair and honest markets
- [ ] Protecting the revenues of this state

**Surplus lines exists to fill a gap, not to let anybody escape the rules that
admitted insurers must meet.** Everything else in the chapter follows from keeping
that gap narrow.

### In what type size must the §981.101(b) warnings appear?

- [x] 11-point
- [ ] 8-point
- [ ] 10-point
- [ ] 14-point

The warnings cover four things: the insurer is **not licensed** in Texas; **TDI
does not audit its finances or review its solvency**; it is **not a guaranty
association member**; and **Chapter 225 requires a stated percentage tax on gross
premium**.

### A surplus lines contract covers a risk located wholly in Texas and contains an arbitration agreement. Where must the arbitration be conducted?

- [x] In Texas, governed by Texas law, unless both agree otherwise after written notice and the insurer gives a premium credit
- [ ] Wherever the insurer's principal office is located
- [ ] In the jurisdiction named in the contract, without qualification
- [ ] In federal court, under the Federal Arbitration Act

**§981.101(d)**. **§981.101(e)** lets the parties agree otherwise by mutual
consent where the insured value exceeds **$2 million**.

### When does Chapter 981 apply?

- [x] Where the home state of the insured is Texas
- [ ] Where the risk is physically located in Texas
- [ ] Where the surplus lines insurer is domiciled in Texas
- [ ] Where the agent's principal office is in Texas

**§981.003.** The home-state test, not the location of the property.

### Who collects and remits the premium tax on a surplus lines placement?

- [x] The agent, who collects it from the insured under Chapter 225
- [ ] The surplus lines insurer, directly to the Comptroller
- [ ] The stamping office, from the filed policy
- [ ] The insured, on their own tax return

The rate is stated on the document itself under §981.101(b), and §981.101(c)
requires the **premium taxes to be collected from the insured** to be shown
alongside the premium and rate.
