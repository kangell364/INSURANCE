### A question refers to "28 TAC § 21.203". Statute or rule, and who made it?

- [x] A rule, in the Texas Administrative Code, adopted by the Commissioner
- [ ] A statute, in the Texas Insurance Code, passed by the Legislature
- [ ] A federal regulation adopted by the NAIC
- [ ] A court decision interpreting the Insurance Code

**The Texas Insurance Code is statute; the Texas Administrative Code, Title 28,
is rules.** A rule has the force of law but cannot exceed the statute it rests
on. "Under Department rules" means the TAC; "under the Insurance Code" means the
TIC.

### May the Commissioner adopt a rule imposing a duty the Insurance Code does not authorise?

- [x] No — Chapter 36 extends to rules necessary to effect the purposes of the code
- [ ] Yes, the Commissioner's rulemaking authority is unlimited
- [ ] Yes, provided the rule is approved by the Legislature afterwards
- [ ] Yes, for matters of consumer protection only

The grant is also the limit. **A rule that goes beyond what the statute
authorises can be challenged.**

### Which body conducts a contested hearing against a licence holder?

- [x] The State Office of Administrative Hearings, under Chapter 40
- [ ] The Texas Department of Insurance
- [ ] The Commissioner personally
- [ ] A district court in Travis County

The point is structural: **the regulator that brings the charge does not also
decide it.** SOAH is an agency independent of TDI.

### Before the Department issues an ordinary cease and desist order under §541.108, what must happen first?

- [x] Investigation, a statement of charges and notice, and a hearing
- [ ] Only a written notice to the licence holder
- [ ] Only an internal determination by the Commissioner
- [ ] Referral to the attorney general

**Step 3 is the one candidates forget.** Except under the emergency procedure in
Chapter 83, **the Department must hold a hearing before it sanctions anybody** —
§541.102 notice, §541.103 hearing, §541.107 determination, §541.108 order.

### Which of these is a statutory duty of the Department under §31.002?

- [x] Administering the workers' compensation system under Title 5, Labor Code
- [ ] Setting rates for all lines of insurance written in Texas
- [ ] Licensing attorneys who practise insurance law
- [ ] Operating the state's residual property market directly

The five duties: regulate the business of insurance; administer workers'
compensation; ensure the code and other insurance laws are executed; protect and
ensure the fair treatment of consumers; and ensure fair competition to foster a
competitive market. **Duty (2) is why Texas workers' compensation appears in an
insurance exam at all.**

### What does §31.002 say about consumer protection and fair competition?

- [x] Both are named as statutory duties, to be pursued together
- [ ] Consumer protection takes precedence over fair competition
- [ ] Fair competition takes precedence over consumer protection
- [ ] Neither is named; both are Department policy rather than statute

The Department is directed to pursue them **together rather than trade one
against the other**.

### Who is the Department's chief executive and administrative officer?

- [x] The Commissioner of Insurance, under §31.021(a)
- [ ] The Governor of Texas
- [ ] The State Office of Administrative Hearings
- [ ] The Texas Insurance Board

§31.021(a) also requires the Commissioner to **administer and enforce this code,
other insurance laws of this state, and other laws granting jurisdiction** over
the department. §31.021(b) extends the same to Title 5 of the Labor Code.

### Under §541.101, what may the Department do to determine whether a prohibited practice has occurred?

- [x] Examine and investigate the affairs of a person engaged in the business of insurance in Texas
- [ ] Revoke the licence immediately, pending investigation
- [ ] Require the person to prove they have not violated the code
- [ ] Refer the matter to the NAIC for determination

Chapter 38 gives the Department its general information-gathering powers, and
Chapter 401 covers examination of insurers' records.

### What does Government Code §2001.051 guarantee?

- [x] An opportunity for hearing and for the presentation of evidence
- [ ] A right to a jury trial in a contested case
- [ ] Confidentiality of all Department investigation materials
- [ ] A right of appeal directly to the Texas Supreme Court

It is the Administrative Procedure Act's guarantee, cited by the blueprint.
Orders are appealed to the courts under **Subchapter D, Chapter 36**.

### What is the general position on the Department's records?

- [x] Public unless a statute makes them confidential, with live investigation materials generally confidential
- [ ] Entirely confidential unless the Commissioner orders release
- [ ] Entirely public, including active investigation files
- [ ] Public only to the parties to a contested case

**Chapter 39** governs public access to Department information, and
**§521.003–.004** the handling of complaints and consumer information.
