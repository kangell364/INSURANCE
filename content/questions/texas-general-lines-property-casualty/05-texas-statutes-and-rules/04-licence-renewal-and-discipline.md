### How long does an agent licence run?

- [x] Two years
- [ ] One year
- [ ] Three years
- [ ] Until the agent's appointment ends

**§4003.001(a)** — expiry falls on the **second anniversary** of issue or renewal
for a non-individual, and on a birthday cycle in an even- or odd-numbered year
for an individual. Either way the period is two years.

### An agent's licence expired 60 days ago. What must they do?

- [x] Renew normally, paying the renewal fee plus one-half of it
- [ ] Obtain a new licence without re-examination
- [ ] Obtain a new licence and re-sit the examination
- [ ] Nothing; the licence remains valid for 90 days

Within **90 days** the licence may still be **renewed**. §4003.007(a) sets the
fee at the renewal fee plus half.

### An agent's licence expired seven months ago. Can they renew it, and must they re-sit the exam?

- [x] They cannot renew, but may obtain a new licence without re-examination
- [ ] They may renew, paying the renewal fee plus one-half
- [ ] They must obtain a new licence and re-sit the examination
- [ ] They may renew only with the Commissioner's written consent

**The word to watch is "renew".** Between 90 days and a year the licence cannot
be renewed at all — a **new** licence is issued, which happens to spare the
applicant the exam. The fee is the licence fee plus half.

### An agent's licence expired 14 months ago. What now?

- [x] A new licence with re-examination, meeting all original-licence requirements
- [ ] Renewal on payment of the renewal fee plus one-half
- [ ] A new licence without re-examination
- [ ] Reinstatement on application to the Commissioner

**Two cliffs: 90 days ends the right to renew, and one year ends the exemption
from re-examination.**

### An agent files a timely renewal application and the Department is slow. Is the agent unlicensed in the meantime?

- [x] No — §4003.006 keeps the original licence in effect until the renewal issues
- [ ] Yes, from the expiry date until the renewal arrives
- [ ] Yes, unless the agent obtains a temporary licence
- [ ] No, but the agent may not write new business

The original licence continues until the renewal issues, the licence is not
renewed for want of continuing education, or the Commissioner revokes it.

### A producer persuades a client to surrender a policy and replace it, using an incomplete comparison. Which ground, and what is the practice called?

- [x] §4005.101(b)(7), and the practice is twisting
- [ ] §4005.101(b)(5), and the practice is churning
- [ ] §4005.101(b)(9), and the practice is rebating
- [ ] §4005.101(b)(6), and the practice is defamation

Ground (7) reaches a statement **misrepresenting or making incomplete comparisons
of a contract to induce the owner to forfeit, surrender or lapse it in order to
replace it**.

### May the Commissioner respond to a violation with anything short of revocation?

- [x] Yes — denial of renewal, suspension, probation, an administrative penalty, a reprimand, or re-qualification
- [ ] No, a proven violation requires revocation
- [ ] Only a monetary penalty is available short of revocation
- [ ] Only where the licence holder consents to the lesser sanction

**The Department is not limited to revocation**, which is why a question offering
"the Commissioner must revoke the licence" is usually wrong.

### May a suspended or revoked licence be renewed?

- [x] No — §4003.004(b) forbids it
- [ ] Yes, on payment of the renewal fee plus one-half
- [ ] Yes, once the suspension period has run
- [ ] Yes, with the written approval of the appointing insurer

Renewal under §4003.004(a) is available only for an **unexpired** licence that is
not suspended or revoked.

### An agent licensed in Texas moves to another state, is licensed there and in continual practice. Their Texas licence has expired. What is available?

- [x] Renewal without re-examination, on paying a fee equal to the licence application fee
- [ ] Nothing; the Texas licence must be obtained afresh with an examination
- [ ] Automatic reciprocity with no fee
- [ ] A temporary licence for 180 days

**§4003.008** is the exception for a producer who moved away and kept practising.

### Which of these is a ground for discipline under §4005.101(b)?

- [x] Conviction of a felony
- [ ] Failure to meet a sales quota set by an appointing insurer
- [ ] Representing more than one insurer
- [ ] Insuring property the agent personally owns

Grounds 1–3 concern **how the licence was obtained**, 4–7 and 9 **how the
producer behaves**, 8 **character**, and 10–11 **controlled business**. Insuring
your own property is expressly permitted by §4001.104(b).

### What does the phrase "individually or through an officer, director, or shareholder" in §4005.101(b) achieve?

- [x] An agency cannot escape discipline by pointing at the individual who did it
- [ ] An individual cannot be disciplined unless the agency is also charged
- [ ] Shareholders are personally liable for the agency's penalties
- [ ] Only officers and directors may be disciplined, not employees

The clause makes the entity answerable for conduct carried out through the people
who run it.

### Where a suspension is probated, what may the Commissioner require under §4005.103?

- [x] Regular reporting, limiting practice to prescribed areas, or continuing professional education until skill is satisfactory
- [ ] Immediate surrender of the licence for the probation period
- [ ] Payment of double the administrative penalty
- [ ] Supervision by another licensed agent at the agent's expense

Probation is a working sanction: the producer keeps trading under conditions
aimed at the deficiency.

### How many continuing education hours must a general property and casualty licence holder complete each period?

- [x] 24, of which three must be ethics
- [ ] 30, of which three must be ethics
- [ ] 24, of which six must be ethics
- [ ] 12, with no ethics requirement

**§4004.053(a)** sets 24 hours and **§4004.054** the three ethics hours. CE is a
condition of renewal — §4003.006(2) refers to a licence not being renewed under
**§4004.055**. The Continuing Education lesson covers the rest.
