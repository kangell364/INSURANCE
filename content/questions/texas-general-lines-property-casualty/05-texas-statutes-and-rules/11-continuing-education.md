### An agent holds both a general property and casualty licence and a general life, accident and health licence. How many CE hours must they complete?

- [x] 24 in total
- [ ] 48 — 24 for each licence
- [ ] 24 for the first licence and 12 for the second
- [ ] 30, because more than one licence is held

**§4004.053(a)** caps the total at **24 hours for all licences** held during the
period. Holding more than one licence does not multiply the requirement.

### How many of those hours must be ethics, and how many in a classroom?

- [x] Three ethics, and at least 12 in a classroom or approved equivalent
- [ ] Three ethics, and at least six in a classroom
- [ ] Six ethics, and at least 12 in a classroom
- [ ] Three ethics, with no classroom requirement

**§4004.054** sets three hours of ethics each renewal period. **§4004.051(c)**
requires **at least 50 percent of all required hours** in a classroom setting or
a classroom-equivalent setting approved by the Department — 12 of 24.

### An agent has held a licence continuously for 22 years. What continuing education do they owe?

- [x] None — §4004.052(b) exempts an agent with at least 20 years of continuous licensure
- [ ] 24 hours, the same as anybody else
- [ ] 12 hours, at half the ordinary requirement
- [ ] Three hours of ethics only

The exemption is for an individual who has **continuously held** an agent licence
issued under the code for at least 20 years.

### An agent claims four hours of credit for active membership of a state insurance association. May those hours satisfy the ethics requirement?

- [x] No — association credit counts toward neither the ethics hours nor the classroom hours
- [ ] Yes, up to all three ethics hours
- [ ] Yes, but only one of the three ethics hours
- [ ] Yes, if the association's programme is certified

**§4004.0535(c)** bars the credit from satisfying either the classroom portion
under §4004.051 or the ethics requirement under §4004.054. Four hours is also the
maximum, and the agent must give the Department a **sworn affirmation** of active
membership.

### An agent's licence period ended on 31 March and they are three hours short. Until when can they cure it?

- [x] The 90th day after the last day of the licensing period
- [ ] The 30th day after the last day of the licensing period
- [ ] The expiration date itself — there is no grace
- [ ] One year after the licence expires

**§4004.055(a)** — the Department **may not renew** if the requirement is not
completed by the 90th day after the period ends, or if a related fine is unpaid.

### An agent completes the missing hours four months after expiry and argues there is now no violation. Is that right?

- [x] No — §4004.055(c) says completion after expiry is not a defence in a disciplinary action
- [ ] Yes, completing the hours cures the violation entirely
- [ ] Yes, provided no complaint had been filed
- [ ] Yes, if the agent pays the associated fine

Catching up may remove an obstacle to a new licence; it does not undo the failure
under §4005.101, §4005.109 or any other provision.

### A limited property and casualty licence holder asks what they owe. What is the answer?

- [x] Five hours annually
- [ ] 24 hours per two-year period
- [ ] 12 hours annually
- [ ] Three hours of ethics only

**§4004.053(b)** treats limited licences differently from the full licences in
subsection (a).

### Who has jurisdiction over continuing education for agents licensed under the Insurance Code?

- [x] The Department, exclusively
- [ ] The Commissioner and the State Office of Administrative Hearings jointly
- [ ] Each appointing insurer, for its own agents
- [ ] The National Association of Insurance Commissioners

**§4004.001** — the Department has **exclusive jurisdiction** of all matters
relating to the continuing education of agents licensed under the code.

### By when must all required hours be completed?

- [x] Before the expiration date of the individual's licence
- [ ] Within 90 days after the expiration date
- [ ] By the end of the calendar year in which the licence expires
- [ ] Within 30 days of the renewal application

**§4004.051(b)** sets the deadline at the expiration date. The 90-day window in
§4004.055(a) is a cure period that blocks renewal if missed, not an extension of
the deadline itself.

### An agent cannot complete the hours because of a serious illness. What is available?

- [x] An extension or exemption on timely written request, under criteria the Commissioner prescribes by rule
- [ ] Automatic waiver on production of a doctor's note
- [ ] Nothing; the requirement is absolute
- [ ] A reduction to half the required hours

**§4004.052(a)** covers **illness, medical disability, or another extenuating
circumstance beyond the agent's control**, and the request must be **timely** and
**in writing**.

### A previously licensed individual lets the licence lapse without completing CE, then applies for a new licence. What does Chapter 4004 require?

- [x] Evidence of completion for the expired licence, and payment of any related fine, before a new licence may issue
- [ ] Nothing; a new licence starts a fresh obligation
- [ ] Double the hours in the first period of the new licence
- [ ] A waiting period of one year

**§4004.055(b)** — **the deficiency follows the person, not the licence.**
Starting again does not wash it off.

### A continuing education programme for adjusters licensed under Chapter 4101 must include education on which of these?

- [x] Chapter 541, Chapter 547, Subchapter A of Chapter 542, and Subchapter E of Chapter 17, Business & Commerce Code
- [ ] Chapter 551 and Chapter 981 only
- [ ] Chapter 4004 and Chapter 4008 only
- [ ] Chapter 2210 and Chapter 2211 only

**§4004.105.** The list names the unfair practices, claims settlement and
deceptive trade practices material an adjuster works under daily.
