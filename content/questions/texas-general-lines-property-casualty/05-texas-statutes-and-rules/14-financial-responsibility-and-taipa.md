### What are the Texas minimum auto liability limits?

- [x] $30,000 per person, $60,000 per accident, $25,000 property damage
- [ ] $25,000 per person, $50,000 per accident, $25,000 property damage
- [ ] $30,000 per person, $60,000 per accident, $30,000 property damage
- [ ] $50,000 per person, $100,000 per accident, $25,000 property damage

**30/60/25**, under **§601.072(a-1)**, effective 1 January 2011. This is the
number the whole Texas auto section rests on.

### An insured wants $50,000 of UM bodily injury coverage on a policy carrying $30,000 of liability. May the insurer write it?

- [x] No — UM may not exceed the policy's own bodily injury liability limits
- [ ] Yes, UM limits are independent of liability limits
- [ ] Yes, up to twice the liability limit
- [ ] Only with the Commissioner's approval

**§1952.105(a)** sets the ceiling at the policy's own limits; **§1952.105(c)** sets
the floor at the Chapter 601 minimums. The insured is boxed in from both sides.

### A driver is in an accident and cannot show financial responsibility. What is the most security the Department may require?

- [x] No more than the limits prescribed by §601.072, and no less than $1,000
- [ ] No more than $1,000
- [ ] Whatever the injured party demands
- [ ] Twice the minimum limits

**§601.154(b)** puts the security between a floor of **$1,000** and a ceiling of
the **minimum limits**.

### A driver has been turned down by one insurer and applies to TAIPA. Eligible?

- [x] No — two rejections are required
- [ ] Yes, one rejection is enough
- [ ] Yes, if the insurer that declined writes auto in Texas
- [ ] Yes, but only for minimum limits

**§2151.102(b)** requires the applicant **and the servicing agent** to certify
rejection by **at least two insurers** that are **authorized to engage in business
in Texas and actually writing automobile insurance** there — the same "authorized
and actually writing" test met in surplus lines and the FAIR Plan.

### How many declinations does each Texas residual market require?

- [x] TWIA one, the FAIR Plan two, TAIPA two
- [ ] TWIA two, the FAIR Plan two, TAIPA one
- [ ] All three require two
- [ ] All three require one

**TWIA is the odd one out** at a single declination. Both property-and-auto plans
that follow require two.

### Who nominates TAIPA's public members, and who selects them?

- [x] Nominated by the Office of Public Insurance Counsel, selected by the commissioner
- [ ] Nominated by the commissioner, elected by association members
- [ ] Elected by association members
- [ ] Appointed by the Governor

**§2151.053(2).** The committee is **15 members**: eight insurer representatives
elected by the members, five public members, and two general or personal lines
property and casualty agents.

### An insurer authorised to write auto liability in Texas declines to join TAIPA. What is the consequence?

- [x] It cannot write auto liability — membership is a condition of its authority
- [ ] It pays a penalty but may continue writing
- [ ] It is assessed at double the ordinary rate
- [ ] Nothing; membership is voluntary

**§2151.051(b).** The same compulsory structure as TWIA and the FAIR Plan: the
insurers that decline the risk collectively carry it.

### To serve on TAIPA's governing committee as an insurer representative, what is required?

- [x] Being a full-time employee of an authorized insurer
- [ ] Holding a general lines agent licence
- [ ] Nomination by the Office of Public Insurance Counsel
- [ ] Ten years of experience in automobile underwriting

**§2151.054.** Compare the FAIR Plan's committee — **11 members: five insurer,
four public, two agents** — against TAIPA's **15: eight, five, two**.

### How are TAIPA's assessments apportioned among member insurers?

- [x] In proportion to each insurer's writing of automobile liability insurance in Texas
- [ ] Equally among all members
- [ ] In proportion to total premium across all lines
- [ ] By the number of TAIPA policies each services

**§2151.103(b).** An insurer that does not pay within a reasonable time may be
sued by the association and reported to the commissioner for a **Chapter 82**
disciplinary action.

### A TAIPA rate filing exceeds 105 percent of the current average rate. What follows?

- [x] A hearing
- [ ] Automatic approval under file and use
- [ ] Automatic disapproval
- [ ] Referral to the Office of Public Insurance Counsel

**§2151.2041.** Ordinary filings may be used under **file and use** (§2151.2021);
the 105 percent threshold is what pulls a filing out of that route.

### What territory must an owner's motor vehicle liability policy cover under §601.076?

- [x] The United States or Canada
- [ ] Texas only
- [ ] The United States, Canada and Mexico
- [ ] Anywhere in the world

The same territory as the personal auto policy in Module 2 — and again,
**not Mexico**.

### A judgment of $90,000 is entered against a driver for one person's bodily injury. How much must be credited before that judgment stops barring reinstatement?

- [x] $30,000 — the §601.072(a-1)(1) amount for one person
- [ ] $90,000 — the whole judgment
- [ ] $60,000 — the per-accident amount
- [ ] $25,000 — the property damage amount

**§601.003.** A judgment larger than the limits is **not wiped out** — the balance
survives as a debt — but it stops being a bar once the statutory amount is
credited.

### Besides an insurance policy, how may financial responsibility be established?

- [x] A surety bond, a deposit with the comptroller or county judge, or self-insurance
- [ ] Only by an insurance policy
- [ ] By a signed affidavit of solvency
- [ ] By membership in TAIPA

**§601.051.** **§601.052** separately lists the exceptions to the requirement.

### What must the standard proof of motor vehicle liability insurance form include?

- [x] Insurer name, policy number, policy period, each insured's name and address, the limits or a compliance statement, and the make and model of each covered vehicle
- [ ] The policy number and the insured's name only
- [ ] The insurer's name and the premium paid
- [ ] A photograph of the vehicle and the driver's licence number

**§601.053.** The form is prescribed by the **Texas Department of Insurance**.
