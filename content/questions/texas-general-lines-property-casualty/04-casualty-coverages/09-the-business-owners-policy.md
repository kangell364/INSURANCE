### A small accounting practice wants property and liability coverage in one policy. Eligible for a BOP, and is anything missing?

- [x] Eligible for the property and general liability, but professional liability and workers' compensation must be bought separately
- [ ] Ineligible; accounting practices cannot be written on a BOP
- [ ] Eligible, and the BOP covers the firm's professional exposure too
- [ ] Eligible only if the firm owns rather than leases its office

A BOP never includes **professional liability**, **workers' compensation**,
**commercial auto**, **liquor liability**, or **flood and earthquake**.

### A restaurant's kitchen fire closes it for four months. Does a BOP pay for the lost trading?

- [x] Yes — business income is included, typically for up to 12 months after a waiting period
- [ ] No, business income must be added to a BOP by endorsement
- [ ] Only the extra expense of operating elsewhere
- [ ] Only if the policy carries a coinsurance percentage of 80 or more

**Business income and extra expense are built in**, commonly with no dollar limit
and no coinsurance. That is the headline difference from a CPP, where both must be
bought.

### A retailer insures a building for $400,000 when it is worth $600,000 and suffers a $100,000 loss. How does a BOP settle it?

- [x] In full, subject to the limit — a BOP has no coinsurance clause
- [ ] Reduced to about 83 percent, as a CPP at 80 percent coinsurance would
- [ ] Reduced to two-thirds, the ratio of carried to actual value
- [ ] Not at all, because the building is underinsured

The form relies instead on an **automatic increase** in the building limit and a
requirement to insure to full value. **This removes the single most common source
of underpayment on a small commercial claim.**

### A business buys a BOP and employs six people. What must it still buy?

- [x] Workers' compensation, and commercial auto if any vehicles are used
- [ ] Nothing; a BOP covers employees and vehicles
- [ ] Only employment practices liability
- [ ] Only commercial auto; a BOP includes employers' liability

**Workers' compensation is always a separate policy, and the BOP covers no
vehicles.**

### Why is an auto repair shop ineligible for a BOP?

- [x] The garage exposure — customers' vehicles in its care — needs an auto dealers form with garagekeepers coverage
- [ ] Repair shops exceed the standard square footage limit
- [ ] Repair shops are classed as light manufacturing
- [ ] Repair shops cannot obtain liability coverage at all

**Anything with a specialised exposure — vehicles, alcohol, professional advice,
heavy manufacturing — is sent to a form built for it.**

### On what basis does the standard BOP cover property?

- [x] Open peril, settled at replacement cost
- [ ] Named peril, settled at actual cash value
- [ ] Open peril, settled at actual cash value
- [ ] Named peril, settled at replacement cost

**Replacement cost is the standard** loss settlement basis on a BOP, with actual
cash value available as an option.

### Which of these is generally ineligible for a BOP?

- [x] A bar
- [ ] A small retail store
- [ ] An apartment building
- [ ] A wholesaler

Generally ineligible: **automobile dealers, repair shops and service stations;
banks and financial institutions; insurance agents; bars and taverns; most
manufacturers; and one- to four-family dwellings**, which belong on personal lines
forms.

### What does the seasonal automatic increase in a BOP do?

- [x] Raises the business personal property limit, commonly by 25 percent, for the stock build-up before a busy season
- [ ] Increases the building limit annually for inflation
- [ ] Extends business income beyond 12 months during peak trading
- [ ] Waives the deductible during the insured's busiest quarter

It answers the retailer whose inventory triples in November and would otherwise be
badly underinsured for two months a year.

### What three limits does an insurer typically cap BOP eligibility with?

- [x] Building size, annual revenue and number of storeys
- [ ] Number of employees, payroll and years in business
- [ ] Building age, construction type and protection class
- [ ] Limit purchased, deductible and loss history

The caps are insurer-specific, but the three dimensions are consistent, and they
are what a producer applies first.

### Which coverages does a BOP commonly include in modest amounts at no extra premium?

- [x] Employee dishonesty, forgery, money and securities, glass, signs and valuable papers
- [ ] Professional liability and employment practices liability
- [ ] Flood and earthquake
- [ ] Commercial auto hired and non-owned liability

It also commonly includes debris removal, fire department service charge, accounts
receivable, pollutant clean-up and newly acquired property for a limited period.

### What is the trade-off a BOP asks the insured to accept?

- [x] Terms are largely fixed and less can be negotiated, in exchange for a lower price and often broader coverage
- [ ] Higher deductibles in exchange for no coinsurance
- [ ] Lower limits in exchange for open peril coverage
- [ ] Annual audits in exchange for a deposit premium

A CPP is assembled part by part, which suits a large or unusual risk and is wasted
effort on a dry cleaner.

### Where does business personal property coverage apply under a BOP?

- [x] At the described premises and within a short distance of them
- [ ] Anywhere in the world
- [ ] At the described premises only
- [ ] Anywhere in the United States and Canada

The building coverage reaches additions, fixtures, permanently installed machinery
and outdoor fixtures at the described premises.
